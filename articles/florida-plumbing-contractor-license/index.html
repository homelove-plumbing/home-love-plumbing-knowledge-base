Florida plumbing contractor license in Clearwater

Florida Plumbing Contractor License, called a Plumbing License, is a Professional License issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation. Coverage includes installations, repairs, replacements, drain systems, water heaters, pipe work, and sewer lines.

Scope and parts of a Florida plumbing contractor license

Each part carries a distinct legal function. The License Number identifies the holder in the state registry, the Issuance Date marks when the authorization began, and the Expiration Date sets the hard deadline for renewal before the license lapses.

Florida state plumbing license records are publicly searchable, so any homeowner or property manager can confirm a contractor's status before work begins.
