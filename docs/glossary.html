Glossary — Home Love Plumbing

Promotional, educational information about Home Love Plumbing. This site is not operated by Home Love Plumbing.

  • Emergency Plumbing Cost Estimation: Emergency plumbing cost estimation is the calculation of potential expenses for urgent repairs.
  • Sewer Line Emergency: A sewer line emergency, called a sewer backup or sewer blockage, is a critical malfunction or blockage in a property's wastewater drainage system that stops or severely restricts the flow of sewage away from the building.
  • Pipe Relining: Pipe relining, called Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP), is a trenchless method of repairing damaged pipes by inserting a resin-saturated liner into the existing pipe and curing it in place to form a new structural pipe wall inside the old one, covering sewer lines, drain lines, supply lines and stormwater pipes in diameters from 50 mm to 300 mm without excavating the ground above them.
  • Leak Detection Equipment: Leak detection equipment, called Leak Detectors or Water Leak Locators, is a category of diagnostic devices used to locate hidden water, gas, or sewage leaks inside walls, under slabs, behind fixtures, and along buried pipe runs without destructive excavation.
  • Drain Snake Equipment: Drain snake equipment, called a plumber's snake or drain auger, is a motorized or hand-cranked auger tool designed to clear obstructions inside drain pipes, including grease buildup, hair clogs, soap accumulation, root intrusions and compacted debris that block water flow through residential and commercial plumbing systems.
  • Pipe Insulation: Pipe insulation, called thermal pipe wrap or pipe lagging, is a material applied directly around water supply lines, drain pipes, refrigerant lines, and other plumbing runs.
  • Water Pressure Regulator Failure: Water pressure regulator failure, called pressure regulator malfunction, is a plumbing problem in which the regulator valve loses its ability to maintain steady water pressure inside a home's supply lines, producing pressure that is too high, too low or wildly inconsistent at every fixture and appliance connected to the system.
  • Pressure Relief Valve: A pressure relief valve, commonly called a PRV, is a mechanical safety device that automatically opens to discharge excess pressure from a plumbing or heating system when internal pressure climbs above a preset threshold, protecting pipes, water heaters, boilers and connected fixtures from rupture, explosion or catastrophic failure.
  • Backflow Prevention Device Failure: Backflow Prevention Device Failure, called Backflow Preventer Malfunction, is a plumbing problem in which a backflow prevention assembly stops doing its job of blocking reverse water flow, allowing non-potable water, chemicals or contaminants to move backward into the clean water supply.
  • Main Water Shutoff Valve: The main water shutoff valve, called the main stopcock, is a water valve that controls the entire water supply entering a building or property, cutting flow to every fixture, appliance and pipe in the system at once.
  • Plumbing Shut Off Valve: A plumbing shut off valve, called a water shutoff valve, is a device installed in plumbing systems to control or stop the flow of water to a fixture, appliance, or an entire supply line.
  • Major Water Leak: A major water leak, called a severe or catastrophic leak, is an uncontrolled, significant discharge of water from a damaged plumbing system.
  • Leaking Pipe: A leaking pipe, called a dripping pipe, is a plumbing condition in which an unintended opening in a pipe wall, joint, seal, or fitting allows water to escape the closed system.
  • Tree Root Intrusion: Tree root intrusion is the penetration and growth of plant roots into underground plumbing pipes, sewer lines and drain systems, causing partial or complete blockages, structural pipe damage, soil displacement and ongoing flow restriction that worsens over time if left untreated.
  • Severe Drain Clog: A severe drain clog, called a major clog or complete drain blockage, is a full or near-full obstruction inside a drainage pipe that reduces water flow to zero or near zero, traps standing water above the blockage, and prevents normal use of any fixture connected to that line.
  • Overflowing Toilet: An overflowing toilet, called a toilet overflow, is a plumbing malfunction where water rises above the bowl rim and spills onto the floor.
  • Running Toilet: A running toilet, called a leaking toilet, is a plumbing malfunction where water flows continuously or intermittently from the tank into the bowl without a flush being triggered.
  • Garbage Disposal Emergency: A garbage disposal emergency, called a Food Waste Disposal Failure, is a sudden, serious malfunction of the under-sink food waste unit that renders it inoperable, creates an active water leak, produces dangerous electrical conditions, or generates a blockage severe enough to back sewage into the sink basin.
  • Flooded Basement: A flooded basement, called an inundated basement, is an undesirable accumulation of water in a building's lowest level.
  • Surface Water Flooding: Surface water flooding, called pluvial flooding, is the accumulation of water on land surfaces from rainfall, runoff and blocked drainage systems that cannot move water away fast enough, leaving standing water across roads, yards, driveways, crawl spaces and building foundations.
  • Gas Leak: A gas leak, called a gas escape, is an unintended escape of fuel gas from a contained plumbing system or appliance.
  • Hot Water Outage: A hot water outage, called a hot water failure, is a complete or partial loss of domestic hot water supply from the heating system.
  • Pipe Corrosion: Pipe corrosion, called metal pipe oxidation, is the deterioration of metal pipes through chemical reactions between the pipe material and its surrounding environment.
  • Flood Cleanup: Flood cleanup, called Flood Remediation, is the removal of standing water, saturated materials, debris, and structural moisture.
  • Florida Plumbing Contractor License: Florida Plumbing Contractor License, called a Plumbing License, is a Professional License issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.
  • Structural Water Damage: Structural water damage, called building water damage, is deterioration of a building's load-bearing components caused by water ingress.
  • After-Hours Plumbing Service: After-hours plumbing service is a type of plumbing service that covers urgent repairs and emergency calls outside standard business hours, including evenings, weekends and holidays, handling problems like burst pipes, sewer backups, water heater failures, drain blockages, active leaks and loss of water pressure when a scheduled appointment is not an option.
