## Leak detection equipment in Clearwater

Leak detection equipment, called Leak Detectors or Water Leak Locators, is a category of diagnostic devices used to locate hidden water, gas, or sewage leaks inside walls, under slabs, behind fixtures, and along buried pipe runs without destructive excavation. These tools belong to the broader class of Diagnostic Equipment. They are among the primary instruments a licensed plumber uses when a leak's location is unknown. ### Measurable attributes of leak detectors

Leak detectors carry several measurable attributes that determine which tool fits a given job. The attributes below are the ones technicians check before selecting equipment for a specific application.

- **Sensitivity:** Sensitivity is expressed in PSI differential or in decibels and sets the smallest leak signal the device can register. High-sensitivity acoustic units can detect a leak producing as little as 0.5 dB above ambient noise.

- **Detection sensitivity:** Detection sensitivity for gas-type leak detectors is rated in parts per million (ppm), with professional-grade units typically resolving concentrations below 1 ppm for tracer gases such as hydrogen-nitrogen blends.

- **Range:** Range is the maximum distance in meters between the sensor and the leak source that still produces a reliable reading. Ground microphone systems used on buried mains commonly carry a rated range of 3 to 10 meters depending on soil density and pipe material.

- **Accuracy:** Accuracy is the percentage deviation between the pinpointed location and the actual leak, with well-calibrated correlator systems achieving accuracy within 5 percent of pipe length.

- **Operating frequency range:** Operating frequency range, measured in Hz to kHz, governs which pipe materials and diameters transmit leak noise effectively. Plastic pipe attenuates high-frequency signals faster than copper, so technicians select units tuned to the lower end of the spectrum, often 100 Hz to 1,200 Hz, for PVC and CPVC runs.

- **Power source:** Power source is either a rechargeable lithium-ion battery for field portability or an AC adapter for stationary correlator units used in a van or utility room.

### Common signs that leak detection equipment is needed

Leak detection equipment is needed when a water bill rises without a change in usage, when a water meter continues to move with all fixtures shut off, when unexplained damp spots appear on walls or floors, or when mold odor develops in a room with no visible moisture source. A pressure drop test is often the first step. A technician isolates the supply line, pressurizes it to its working PSI, and watches the gauge over 15 to 30 minutes. A falling reading confirms a breach somewhere in the system, but it does not locate it. That is the point where acoustic or thermal leak detection equipment enters the workflow.[Major water leak](https://home-love-plumbing.mintlify.site/major-water-leak) situations frequently require this equipment before any repair begins.

### What leak detection equipment is not

Leak detection equipment is not Leak Repair Equipment, Blocking Tools, or Leak Prevention Systems. The distinction is deliberate. Leak prevention systems, such as automatic shutoff valves and whole-house flow monitors, are installed to stop future leaks from causing damage, not to find an existing one. Professional leak detection equipment occupies a distinct role: it is a diagnostic instrument only, and its output is a location, not a repair. A technician who skips the detection step and opens a wall based on guesswork risks missing the leak entirely, extending both the job and the water damage.
